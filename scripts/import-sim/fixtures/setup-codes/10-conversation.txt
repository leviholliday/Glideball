Here's the plan we landed on, read back one more time:

- **Top left** (the one you never use) becomes your Wispr Flow key: hold it, talk, let go.
- **Top right** stays Back when you tap it, but hold it and roll to switch desktops (left and right), Mission Control (up) and App Exposé (down). If you pause, a little ring shows what's where. While you hold it, the scroll ring turns the volume.
- **Both top buttons together** play or pause your music.
- **In Figma only:** a slower pointer, the page follows the scroll ring and goes further per tick, and bottom right becomes a "hold for precision" button for nudging things.
- **Steady hands** is on, so a wobble while you hold a button won't count as a roll.
