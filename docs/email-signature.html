Putting it in Proton Mail

  1. Press Copy under the version you like.
  2. In Proton Mail on the web, open Settings → Identity and addresses (or Accounts and addresses), and pick glideball@proton.me.
  3. Click into the Signature box, delete what's there, and paste (⌘V). Save.
  4. Send yourself a test email to check it.

The icon is loaded from the Glideball website, so it always matches the current app icon. Some mail apps (Proton included) hold back pictures until the reader allows them; the text and links still show either way.
