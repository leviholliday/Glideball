Putting it in Proton Mail

  1. Press Copy under the look you want.
  2. In Proton Mail on the web, open Settings → Identity and addresses and pick glideball@proton.me.
  3. Click in the Signature box, select everything that's there (⌘A) and delete it, then paste (⌘V). Save.
  4. Start a new email to check it: the line should run across the whole message.

The icon loads from the Glideball website. Some mail apps hold back pictures until the reader allows them; the text and links show either way.
