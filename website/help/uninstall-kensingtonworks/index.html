How to uninstall KensingtonWorks

To uninstall KensingtonWorks, run the uninstaller Kensington provides, then restart your Mac. Do this before you install Glideball, because KensingtonWorks's driver and Glideball both try to handle your trackball's buttons.

A note before you start. I'm not Kensington, and I haven't checked every KensingtonWorks version. Where its uninstaller lives and what it's called may differ on your Mac. If Kensington's own instructions say something different, follow theirs.

Uninstall it

  1. Quit KensingtonWorks

    If it has a menu bar icon or a window open, quit it.

  2. Run the KensingtonWorks uninstaller

    Look in your Applications folder for an app named something like KensingtonWorks Uninstaller (sometimes inside a KensingtonWorks folder). Open it and follow its steps. Older setups may call it TrackballWorks.

    Can't find one? The KensingtonWorks installer you downloaded from kensington.com may include it. Just dragging the app to the Trash can leave the driver behind.

  3. Restart your Mac

    Don't skip this one. The driver can keep running until you restart.

  4. Check nothing is left

    Open System Settings › General › Login Items & Extensions and look for anything from Kensington. Then check Privacy & Security › Accessibility and Input Monitoring, and remove KensingtonWorks from those lists if it's still there.

How to tell it worked

After the restart, your trackball should act like a plain mouse: the ball moves the pointer, the scroll ring scrolls, and each button does one ordinary thing. That's the right starting point for Glideball.

If a button does two things at once after you install Glideball, part of KensingtonWorks is probably still running. Go through the steps above again, and restart once more.

Next

Download Glideball, drag it into Applications, and open it with Open Anyway. Your KensingtonWorks settings don't carry over, but Quick assign on the Buttons tab gets you set up fast.

Still stuck? Tell me what happened on the feedback page or email glideball@proton.me. In the app, Help › Send Feedback… can attach your settings and a scroll log, and you see it all before it goes. It's just me, so replies can take a few days.
