Help

Uninstall Glideball completely

To uninstall Glideball, quit it, drag it to the Trash, and remove it from Accessibility and Input Monitoring. To remove every setting too, delete two folders and run one Terminal command.

Remove the app

  1. Turn off Open at login, then quit

    Turn off Glideball › Settings › General › Open at login. Then press ⌘Q, or use the menu bar icon › Quit Glideball. Your trackball goes back to normal right away.

  2. Drag Glideball to the Trash

    From your Applications folder.

  3. Remove its permissions

    In System Settings › Privacy & Security, select Glideball in Accessibility and in Input Monitoring, and click the minus button in each.

Remove your settings too (optional)

Glideball was called Glide until 2.8, and its folders kept that name. In Finder, choose Go › Go to Folder… and delete these:

  • ~/Library/Application Support/Glide (automatic backups)
  • ~/Library/Logs/Glide
  • iCloud Drive › Glide, if you turned on sync

Then run this in Terminal to clear your settings:

defaults delete com.leviholliday.glide

If you might come back later, keep a copy first with File › Export Settings… (⌘E).

Installed with Homebrew?

Quit Glideball first, then run:

brew uninstall --zap --cask glideball

The --zap part removes its settings, backups and logs as well. It leaves the iCloud Drive › Glide folder, so delete that yourself if you used sync.

Going back to KensingtonWorks?

Uninstall Glideball first, then install KensingtonWorks from kensington.com. If you have a minute, I'd love to hear what didn't work for you on the feedback page.
