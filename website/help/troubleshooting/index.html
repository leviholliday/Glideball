Troubleshooting

Need your normal trackball back right now? Press ⌃⌥⌘G (Control-Option-Command-G) anywhere to pause Glideball, or quit it. Then find your problem below.

Common problems

My buttons do two things at once

KensingtonWorks is probably still installed. Its driver and Glideball both act on the same click. Uninstall KensingtonWorks and restart your Mac.

The permissions are on, but nothing changes

In System Settings › Privacy & Security, remove Glideball from Accessibility and Input Monitoring, add it back, and restart Glideball. Or run this in Terminal, then restart Glideball and allow both again:

tccutil reset All com.leviholliday.glide
Glideball doesn't see my trackball

The wired Expert Mouse is always picked up. Other Kensington trackballs (and the wireless Expert Mouse, depending on the name it reports) need Other Kensington trackballs turned on: click Turn On when Glideball offers it, or turn it on in Glideball › Settings › General. Trackballs from other brands aren't supported. See supported trackballs.

Where did the window go?

Closing the window keeps Glideball running. Click Glideball in the Dock, or use the menu bar icon › Show Glideball.

How do I quit it?

Press ⌘Q in Glideball's window, or use the menu bar icon › Quit Glideball. In 2.9.1, Quit in the Dock is ignored on purpose. The 2.10 beta makes it work.

Scrolling feels wrong

Try another mode on the Scrolling tab. Flywheel coasts after each tick, Follow stops when you stop, and Native leaves it to macOS. In Flywheel, the Kensington feel button puts back values measured from Kensington's own driver.

A button does something I didn't set

In 2.9.1, the top buttons switch Spaces out of the box, and pressing bottom-left plus both top buttons holds ⌃Space for dictation apps. Set any button back to Default on the Buttons tab, or delete the combo. In the 2.10 beta, new installs start with normal buttons.

An update seems stuck

Quit Glideball and open it again. If that doesn't help, download the latest Glideball.zip and replace the copy in Applications. Your settings stay.

Does it use much CPU or battery?

Very little since 2.9.1. To use even less, set Glideball › Settings › General › Performance to Efficient or Minimal.

Why do some files say “Glide”?

That was the app's name until 2.8. Its settings and folders kept the old name.

Sending a good bug report

Help › Send Feedback… in the app is the best way. You can attach your settings and a scroll log (scroll and button timings, no keystrokes), each with its own switch, and you see everything before it's sent. Say what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.

Still stuck? Tell me what happened on the feedback page or email glideball@proton.me. In the app, Help › Send Feedback… can attach your settings and a scroll log, and you see it all before it goes. It's just me, so replies can take a few days.
