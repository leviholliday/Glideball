What's new in Glideball

The current release is 2.9.1. Glideball checks for updates by itself, and a button with the new version number shows up at the top of the window when one is out.

In beta: 2.10

These builds go to people who turned on Early updates in the app (Glideball › Settings › Updates › Early updates). They become the next release once they've had some use.

7 beta builds of 2.10, newest first

November 11, 2026

• What's New, Help and Troubleshooting now open the Glideball website, and there's a "Why?" link next to the permission requests. • The launch chime now only plays the first time Glideball opens after an update. Settings has a new "When Glideball opens" choice: Animation, Quick fade or Nothing. Help › Play the Intro replays the intro. • ⌘1 to ⌘6 switch pages (see the new View menu), and Escape closes Settings. • Behind the scenes: every release is now tested before it goes out, including a check that it launches.

Glide 2.6

October 4, 2026

A grand entrance.

  • New launch animations: every time you start Glide you get one of six short animations, never the same twice in a row. First-time users get a longer intro with original music, leading into the Welcome Tour. Turn sounds off with 'Launch sounds' in Overview > General.
  • Pages now slide between tabs, cards rise in, and today's counters count up.
  • Check for Updates: a button in Overview > General, plus 'Check for Updates…' in the Glide menu and the menu-bar menu.

Glide 2.5

October 4, 2026

Combos, shortcuts and a little celebration.

  • Three-button combos are easier: tick buttons in the new 'New combo' row (Buttons tab), and pressing three buttons together is more forgiving.
  • Keyboard shortcuts (Overview): toggle Precision, Scroll with ball and Drag lock from the keyboard, and change the pause shortcut (Control-Option-Command-G by default). All optional.
  • Your trackball: lifetime totals with real-world comparisons, a 7-day chart, personal records and 17 badges to earn, celebrated once with a little confetti.
  • 'Make it yours' checklist to set Glide up your way.
  • Button ripples and a ring trail on the live trackball, and a 'Saved' check when settings apply.
  • Celebrations and sounds can be turned off in Overview > General (sounds are off by default).

Glide 2.4

October 4, 2026

Six new features.

  • Precision mode: hold (or toggle) a button to slow the cursor for pixel-perfect aim. Set the precision speed on the Pointer tab.
  • Scroll with the ball: hold a button and roll the ball to scroll in any direction while the cursor stays put; let go mid-roll and it glides.
  • Drag lock: press once to grab, roll to drag, press again (or click) to drop. No more holding a button while dragging.
  • App setups: give any app its own pointer speed, scrolling, buttons and combos. Glide switches the moment that app comes to the front.
  • Welcome Tour for new users (Help > Show Welcome Tour).
  • Beta program (Overview > General): try Glide with other Kensington trackballs and get early test builds. Off by default.

Safety: pausing (Control-Option-Command-G), quitting or unplugging always undoes precision, ball scrolling and drag lock.

Glide 2.1

October 4, 2026

One-click updates.

Glide 2.0

October 4, 2026

First public release of Glide for the Kensington Expert Mouse.

  • Pointer speed past macOS's limit, applied only to the Expert Mouse
  • Three scroll modes: Native, Flywheel (tuned from Kensington's own driver), Follow
  • Remap buttons, two- and three-button combos, quick assign, hold-to-talk shortcuts
  • Live dashboard with graphs and an animated trackball
  • Backup & Sync: export/import settings files and sync across Macs through iCloud Drive
  • Safety: the main click is always a left click; Control-Option-Command-G pauses Glide instantly

Install: unzip, move Glide to Applications, then open it. Because Glide isn't notarized, macOS may ask you to allow it in System Settings > Privacy & Security (Open Anyway). Grant Accessibility and Input Monitoring when asked. Universal: Apple silicon and Intel, macOS 26 or later.
