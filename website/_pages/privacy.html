The app

Here's every time Glideball uses the network:

  • Update checks. At launch and then once a day, it asks GitHub whether there's a newer version. GitHub sees that request like any other web request.
  • Downloading an update, from GitHub, when you choose Install.
  • iCloud sync, only if you turn it on. Your settings go to your own iCloud Drive. Sync never goes through a Glideball server.
  • Feedback, only when you press Send. You can review it first. Your settings and the scroll log (scroll and button timings, no keystrokes) are attached only if you leave their switches on.

That's all. Your activity totals (clicks, distances, personal bests) stay on your Mac. Glideball doesn't see what you type; the permissions page explains exactly what it does see.

Feedback you send

Feedback from the app or the feedback page is stored privately on this website's host, Netlify, and only I can read it. My phone gets a notification with its subject line.

This website

  • Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits without cookies.
  • Google Analytics runs only if you accept it. If you don't, it isn't loaded.
  • No advertising features.

You can change your choice any time with Privacy settings at the bottom of every page. The full notice is there too, under Website privacy and analytics.
