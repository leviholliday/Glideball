Which Kensington trackballs work with Glideball?

  • Kensington Expert Mouse, wired (USB)

    Fully supported

    The one Glideball is built and tested on. Everything works: pointer speed, all three scroll-ring modes, all four buttons and combos.

  • Kensington Expert Mouse Wireless

    Works with Other Kensington trackballs on

    It may show up as fully supported, depending on the name it reports. If Glideball says Beta, turn on Other Kensington trackballs.

  • Kensington SlimBlade

    Works with Other Kensington trackballs on

    Not tuned yet. The top buttons aren't supported yet.

  • Kensington Orbit trackballs

    Works with Other Kensington trackballs on

    Not tuned yet.

  • Other Kensington trackballs and mice

    Works with Other Kensington trackballs on

    Glideball treats any Kensington pointing device as a Beta device.

  • Other brands, the trackpad, the Magic Mouse

    Not yet

    Never touched. They keep their System Settings.

What Beta means here

Glideball is built and tested on the wired Expert Mouse, so that's the one I can vouch for. Glideball still knows the other Kensington trackballs, but it won't change them unless you ask. When it sees one, it offers Turn On. You can also turn on Glideball › Settings › General › Other Kensington trackballs.

That switch only lets Glideball work with Kensington's other trackballs. Early builds of the app are a separate switch, Early updates, in Settings › Updates. Both are set on each Mac separately and never sync.

If you try Glideball on one of these, please tell me how it went, good or bad. That's how a model moves to Full.

What about other brands?

Glideball only works with Kensington trackballs right now. I'd like to support other brands someday, but I can't promise when I'll get to it. Until then, Glideball leaves them alone.

How Glideball decides

It goes by the USB vendor ID. Kensington is 047D. A device with product ID 1020, or with “Expert Mouse” in its name, counts as the Expert Mouse. Any other Kensington pointing device is a Beta device. Everything else is left alone. The rule is in DeviceSupport.swift.

The Linux and Windows previews follow the same rule.
